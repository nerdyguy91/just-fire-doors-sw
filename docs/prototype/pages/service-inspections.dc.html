05 · Frequency

How often should fire doors be inspected?

There isn’t one useful answer that can be copied onto every building.

The appropriate inspection and checking regime can depend on factors including the type and use of the building, applicable requirements, the condition and usage of the doors and the level of risk involved.

Routine checks may also be different from a more detailed inspection carried out by a competent person.

JFD can help you understand the condition of your current fire doors and, where appropriate, establish a more manageable inspection and maintenance cycle going forward.
